Our History

uZuri Skincare Clinic was established in 2024 in Richardsbay, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. Founded by Siphesihle Madlala, a young skincare specialist with the vision to offer luxury skincare treatments at an affordable price. The clinic began as a small aesthetic practice specialising in facials and acne treatments and has since expanded to include chemical peels, microneedling, laser therapy, and advanced anti-aging treatments.

The business is currently located at Junction 12 Shopping Centre, Bullion Blvd, Richardsbay Central. In 2025, uZuri Skincare Clinic was recognised at the Aesthetic Medicine Awards as the most innovative upcoming female-owned beauty clinic. That same year, uZuri collaborated with L'Oréal to create a new range focusing on quality skincare products for people with conditions such as eczema. uZuri Skincare Clinic is a proud member of the Dermatology Society of South Africa (DASSA).

Our Mission

To provide quality skincare treatments at an affordable price, making luxury skincare accessible to everyone regardless of their budget.

Our Vision

We envision a world where everyone has access to skincare treatments at an affordable price. Everyone deserves to look and feel good. At uZuri we plan on making skincare treatment accessible to all. Our focus is not only enhancing individual beauty, but also empowering the youth and creating job opportunities within our community.

Our Team

We are a group of qualified skincare specialists and credible registered nurses. We are passionate about our craft and believe in providing only the highest quality skincare services. Our team stays up to date with the latest techniques and products to ensure you receive the best possible care.
